Infrastructure / Recovery / Service Overview
Snapshot date: 2026-09-27

PURPOSE
-------
Clanker is Kyle's primary always-on Ubuntu VPS. It acts as a mixed application
host, protocol server, VPN/router, observability node, Yggdrasil overlay node,
DN42 edge router, reverse-proxy host, authoritative DNS host, local recursive/
caching resolver, and backup peer/storage node.

The DN42 role is now a real multi-router design rather than a single-host stub:
Clanker is edge/router #1 for AS4242420425 and has a dedicated inter-router
WireGuard core plus iBGP to ScopeCreep, which is being developed as router/edge
#2.

This file is intended to answer:
- What is Clanker?
- What runs here?
- What starts it?
- Where is its configuration?
- Where is persistent data?
- Which secrets and identities matter for recovery?
- What networks does it participate in?
- What backup responsibilities does it have?
- What would be needed to rebuild it?
- What remains worth documenting or cleaning up?

It is deliberately broader than a backup manifest.

and /128 routes remain internal.

The next major DN42 topology step is a direct external peer on ScopeCreep.
